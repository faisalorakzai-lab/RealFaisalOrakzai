Frequently Asked Questions — Faisal Orakzai & Orakzai Group

Who is Faisal Orakzai?
Muhammad Faisal Orakzai (فیصل اورکزئی) is a Pakistani technology entrepreneur born on 30 April 2006 in Orakzai Agency, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KPK), Pakistan. He is the Founder and Chairman of Orakzai Group, creator of Orakzai Bond (OKBOND) on Polygon blockchain, OkzByte Technology, Shamim Forever luxury brand, and OrakzaiX AI.
What is Orakzai Group?
Orakzai Group is a technology and venture organization founded by Faisal Orakzai, operating across blockchain, AI, fintech, luxury commerce, and digital infrastructure. Ventures include Orakzai Bond (OKBOND), OkzByte Technology, Shamim Forever, OrakzaiX AI, QORIX, and Orakzai Real Estate. Headquartered in Karachi, Pakistan.
What is Orakzai Bond (OKBOND)?
Orakzai Bond (OKBOND) is a blockchain-based digital asset built on the Polygon Layer-2 network by Faisal Orakzai. It is a sovereign tokenized digital asset exploring transparent treasury systems, staking infrastructure, and DeFi yield mechanisms. Available at orakzaibond.com.
Where is Faisal Orakzai from?
Faisal Orakzai is from Orakzai Agency (Tirah Valley), Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KPK), Pakistan. He operates from Karachi. He is of Pashtun heritage from the Orakzai tribe.
How old is Faisal Orakzai?
Faisal Orakzai was born on 30 April 2006, making him 20 years old as of 2026. He is one of Pakistan's youngest founders in blockchain and AI.

What is Orakzai Bond on Polygon blockchain?
Orakzai Bond (OKBOND) is deployed on Polygon Layer-2 — a scalable, low-fee Ethereum-compatible network providing fast settlement and DeFi compatibility. It is described as a sovereign tokenized debt instrument in the Orakzai Bond white paper.
What is OkzByte Technology?
OkzByte Technology (OKZBYTE) is a technology venture under Orakzai Group providing web development, AI automation, blockchain integration, enterprise software, and digital transformation services in Pakistan and internationally.
What is Shamim Forever?
Shamim Forever is a Pakistani luxury fashion and lifestyle brand founded by Faisal Orakzai under Orakzai Group. It blends premium craftsmanship with digital storytelling and Pakistani cultural heritage. Available at shamimforever.com.
What is OrakzaiX AI?
OrakzaiX AI is an artificial intelligence automation platform and research framework under Orakzai Group. It develops AI workflow automation, AI agents, LLM integrations, and AI infrastructure for Pakistani and global markets.
